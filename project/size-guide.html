Size Guide

Use the chart below to find your correct size. Measure around the bust, waist, and hips, and compare with the chart.

Size Bust (cm) Waist (cm) Hips (cm) US Equivalent
XS 76–81 58–63 83–88 0–2
S 82–87 64–69 89–94 4–6
M 88–93 70–75 95–100 8–10
L 94–99 76–81 101–106 12–14
XL 100–105 82–87 107–112 16–18

Tip: If you are between sizes, choose the larger size for comfort.
