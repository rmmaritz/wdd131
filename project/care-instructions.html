Dance Clothing Care Guide

Proper care ensures your dancewear lasts longer and keeps its shape. Follow these steps:

  • Washing: Hand wash in cold water with mild detergent. Avoid bleach and fabric softeners.
  • Drying: Lay flat to dry. Do not tumble dry or wring out garments.
  • Ironing: Use a cool iron if necessary. Avoid direct heat on elastic or printed areas.
  • Storage: Store in a cool, dry place. Keep away from direct sunlight to prevent fading.
  • Tights: Wash inside out to prevent snags. Be careful with nails and jewelry when putting them on.
  • Shoes: Wipe with a damp cloth after use. Do not machine wash or soak.

Tip: Always check the garment’s label for specific care instructions.
